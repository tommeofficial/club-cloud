Good to know

What counts as using the decks?

Deck time only runs while a deck is actually playing. Browsing, chatting and watching streams never use it.

Do my hours run out?

No. Hours you buy stay on your account until you use them.

What happens when my time runs out mid-set?

You get a two-minute grace period with the music still playing, and a one-tap button to add an hour.

Have a promo code?

Open the deck time screen in the app and enter it in the box marked "Have a code?".

Can I play any music when I go live?

You confirm you have the rights to broadcast what you play. Read the terms.
