ABOUT CLUB CLOUD

The world's first DJ club
simulator and live stream.

Mix on a full set of decks in your browser, watch a crowd of people react to every drop, and put on a show for a live audience. No kit needed, just a track and a beat.

What you can do

PLAY THE DECKS

Two decks and a mixer with waveforms, cue points, loops, sync, effects and crossfader. Load your own tracks and the app finds the tempo, key and beat grid for you.

A CROWD THAT REACTS

A crowd of 3D dancers moves to the beat of what you play. They cheer on drops, put their hands up when the lasers come in, and boo a train-wreck mix.

A REAL LIGHT SHOW

Moving heads, washes, lasers and CO2 cannons run in time with your music through haze, with a big LED screen behind the stage. Choose Chill, Energetic or Rave.

TWO CLUB CAMERAS

Watch from the back of the room, or let camera B cut between shots on the beat. The DJ view puts you right behind the decks.

GO LIVE

Pro DJs broadcast their set to up to 12 listeners straight from their browser. Listeners see the same club, lights and beat as the DJ, in time with the music.

CHAT AND REQUESTS

Every room has live chat. Send a tip, request a song, ask for a shout-out on the big screen, or book a short video meet and greet. DJs set their own prices.

BRING YOUR OWN SOUND

Plug in a mic or a line from real decks and the club follows it: it finds the tempo, keeps the beat, and runs the lights and crowd from what it hears.

RECORD YOUR SET

Pro members can record their mix and download it as a high-quality file.
