What we use

Club Cloud uses cookies and similar browser storage.

  • Essential (always on): keeps you signed in, remembers deck time and settings, and makes payments work. The service cannot run without these.
  • Optional: none at the moment. If we add analytics or marketing tools we will only turn them on if you accept.

Your choice

When you first visit you can accept or decline optional cookies. You can change your mind any time with Cookie settings, or by clearing your browser's site data.
